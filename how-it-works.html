Secret & Credential Detection

ShieldVault intercepts paste, typed input, submit and file-drop events. If a pattern matches, the content is wiped before it reaches the page and you see a "Blocked" overlay. Clicking "Undo (allow once)" restores the content if it was a false positive.

Soft Flag (Off by default)

Behavioral & Tone Warnings

When enabled in Settings, ShieldVault shows a pause modal before you send messages that may contain heated language. You can always click "Send Anyway" — it is never a hard block.

Known limits

⚠ShieldVault only watches sites listed in its permissions. Custom or enterprise AI tools on unlisted domains are not covered.
⚠Short or heavily obfuscated secrets may not be detected — ShieldVault uses pattern matching, not AI inference.
⚠If a site uses a non-standard editor (e.g. CodeMirror canvas-rendered), detection may not intercept every keystroke path.
⚠Phone number detection may produce false positives on numeric data that looks like phone numbers.
