💡 Why some toggles are off

We've calibrated defaults to catch what matters and skip what doesn't. SSN and Credit Card detection are on by default because accidental leaks have real cost. Phone and Home Address are off because people usually share those on purpose — detection there creates more false alarms than real protection. Late Night Guard is off by default — it's a power feature best enabled once you know ShieldVault.

Turn anything on if you want extra coverage. Turn anything off if it gets in your way.
